Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Warframe Trade Watch.

What is Warframe Trade Watch?

Warframe Trade Watch is a desktop application for players who actively trade on Warframe Market. It continuously monitors public market listings and notifies you when matching buy or sell orders appear.

Does Warframe Trade Watch require a Warframe Market account?

No. Warframe Trade Watch monitors public market listings directly and works without any Warframe Market login or browser session.

Can I monitor multiple items?

Yes. You can track dozens of items simultaneously instead of searching one at a time on the website.

Does Warframe Trade Watch work on Windows, macOS, and Linux?

Yes. Warframe Trade Watch is a cross-platform desktop app available on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Does Warframe Trade Watch modify Warframe?

No. Warframe Trade Watch does not modify the Warframe client, interact with Digital Extremes servers, or automate in-game actions. It is a passive notification client for an external community website.

Can I monitor both buy and sell orders?

Yes. Warframe Trade Watch can monitor both WTS sell orders and WTB buy orders on Warframe Market with price and rank filters.

How do Warframe Market notifications work?

When a matching order appears on Warframe Market, Warframe Trade Watch shows a desktop notification and plays a sound so you can act immediately while playing.
