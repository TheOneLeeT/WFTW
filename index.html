How it works

Three simple steps. No setup headaches.

Choose your items

Add the items you want to watch and set your price and rank filters for buy or sell orders.

Let it run in the background

The app monitors Warframe Market continuously while minimized to your system tray.

Get notified

Receive an instant desktop notification the moment a matching order appears. Copy the seller or buyer name and trade on your terms.
